    How a score is calculated – Algihaz's existing rule, carried over as-is

    On-time submission (within a 4-day grace period) = full point. Deliverables not yet due are excluded. Late submissions taper down; anything over 28 days late counts as not submitted.

    On time → 100% 1–7d late → 90% 8–14d → 80% 15–21d → 70% 22–28d → 60% >28d → 0%

    Choose which catalog items count toward on-time performance tracking. Turning an item off excludes it everywhere — department scores, Top Achievers, everything — for every project, past and future. Milestones stay on; they anchor the due-date chain regardless.
