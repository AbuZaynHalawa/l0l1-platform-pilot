      Department Performance

      Live approval rate per department, this pilot.

      How the Performance % is calculated

      1. Submitted Deliverables (On Time) equals = 1 Point
      2. Submitted Deliverables (Early, before the Due Date) earn a 10% bonus = 1.1 Points
      3. Not Submitted Deliverables equals = 0 Point
      4. POs points will be calculated on a pro rata basis (Eg: 5 out of 10 POs submitted equals = 0.5 Point)
      5. Deliverables that are Not Due are excluded from the calculation
      6. Delay Tolerance of 4 days is used (Delay is calculated from Day 5 after Due Date)
      7. Late Deliverables (Submitted after Day 4) will have a penalty as follows:
        1–7 Days Delay → -10% (0.9 Point) 8–14 Days Delay → -20% (0.8 Point) 15–21 Days Delay → -30% (0.7 Point) 22–28 Days Delay → -40% (0.6 Point) >28 Days Delay → -100% (0 Point / Considered not Submitted)
      8. L0 and L1 Performance % = weighted average, across each catalog item in the department, of (Submitted items for that item / Due items for that item) — each item's own average counts once, so an item due on more projects doesn't automatically outweigh one due on fewer.
      9. Every item counts equally by default. An Admin can give one item more Scoring Weight than its siblings via Deliverables Configuration (e.g. weighting BOQ higher than a less critical item) — see the item's weight on Deliverables Catalog.
      10. A department's overall % is capped at 100% — early-submission bonus points can offset lates within a department, but can't push the reported score past fully on track.
